⚠️ Important Disclaimer — Please Read

Leave calculations are indicative only. Accrual rules are based on Australian Fair Work Act standards for each employment type. Casual workers do not accrue paid leave. Always verify your actual entitlements with your HR or payroll team — enterprise agreements and awards may vary.

ADO (Accrued Days Off) limits: Some organisations cap ADO balances at a maximum of 5 days or 10 days at any given time. Once you reach this cap, further ADO may not accrue until the balance is drawn down. Please check with your manager or HR team to confirm your organisation's specific ADO cap before planning leave that relies on a higher balance.

Visa information is general guidance based on publicly available policy as of 2025. Visa rules change frequently — always verify with the official embassy or government immigration website before booking. This is not legal or immigration advice.

Flight prices are not estimated. The Skyscanner links open live search results — real current fares, not fabricated numbers.
